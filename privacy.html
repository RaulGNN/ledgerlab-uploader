Privacy Policy

Ledger Lab Uploader · Last updated: November 1, 2026

Ledger Lab Uploader ("the app", also called Channel Studio) is a tool that the owner of the Ledger Lab channels runs on their own computer to upload and schedule their own videos on YouTube, Instagram and TikTok. This policy explains what data the app accesses on those platforms and how it is handled.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By authorizing it you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and your data is subject to the Google Privacy Policy.

Data the app accesses

  • Permission to upload videos (youtube.upload): used to upload video files the owner produced and to set their title, description, tags, category and scheduled publish time.
  • Read-only access to the authorized channel (youtube.readonly): used only to read the channel's name and ID, so the app can confirm each video is sent to the intended channel.
  • Instagram (instagram_business_basic, instagram_business_content_publish): reads the account's ID and username to confirm the destination, and publishes Reels the owner produced, with their caption.
  • TikTok (user.info.basic, video.upload, video.publish): reads the account's open ID and display name to confirm the destination, and sends videos the owner produced to the account, either as a draft in the TikTok inbox or as a post.

The app does not access comments, followers, subscribers, analytics, messages, contacts or any other account data.

How data is stored

  • OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer, in a file readable only by that user. The app has no server and no database.
  • The app keeps a local log of uploaded video IDs and publish times so it does not upload the same video twice.

Sharing

The app does not sell, share, transfer or disclose any Google, YouTube, Instagram or TikTok data to anyone. Data is sent only to each platform's own API to perform the uploads. It is not used for advertising, profiling or training models.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access and deleting data

You can revoke the app's access at any time: for Google on the Google security settings page, for Instagram in the app's Settings under "Apps and websites", and for TikTok in Settings and privacy under "Security" and "Manage app permissions". Deleting the local token file removes all stored credentials. Videos already uploaded remain on YouTube and can be managed or deleted in YouTube Studio.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
